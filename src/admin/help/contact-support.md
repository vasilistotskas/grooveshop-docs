# When to contact support

You do most things yourself from the admin. Some, though, concern every store on the platform or the security of your customers, and only the platform team does them. This page says which ones and what to send.

## What you see

Screens that only the platform team opens do not appear in your menu. For each case below, the table says what to try first and what to ask for.

| Case | Try first | Ask the platform team |
|---|---|---|
| A customer lost the phone with their sign-in code (two-step verification) | If they kept recovery codes, they sign in with one of them. | To remove two-step verification from their account, after you have made sure it really is the customer. |
| A customer does not receive the confirmation code for their email | They ask for a new code on the page and check their spam folder. | To confirm the email address, after you have made sure it is theirs. |
| A customer wants to be signed out on all their devices | In their storefront account, under signed-in devices, they click "Sign out everywhere else". | To sign them out everywhere, if they cannot get in themselves. |
| You want to see what changed on an order or in a product's stock | The **Order History** tab on the order's page and the **Stock activity** tab on the product's page. | A check of a payment that was not recorded or of stock that does not add up. Send the order number or the product code (SKU). |
| The money for an ACS cash-on-delivery order has not arrived | ACS usually pays out a few days after delivery. After ten days you get an email listing the vouchers (the shipping labels): ask ACS about them. If you were paid another way, click **Record COD received outside ACS** on the shipment's page. | If ACS says it paid but the order did not become paid, to record the payout again. Send the voucher numbers. |
| You want receipt numbers to continue from a previous system | — | To set the next number **before** the year's first receipt is issued. Send the last number you used. |
| A wrong country or region name in the checkout address | — | To correct it. Countries and regions are shared by every store. |
| A wrong or untranslated word on an admin screen, in an email or on the storefront | — | To correct the translation. Send which screen it is on and the word. |
| A customer asks about the export of their data | The file stays available to the customer for seven days after it is ready. | To check whether the export was ready, failed or expired. Send the customer's email. |
| You want the storefront in English too | Translate the legal pages first, see [Languages & translations](/admin/settings/languages-and-translations). | To switch the language on. |
| You want an automatic task to run sooner or at another time | See the buttons in [Automatic tasks](/admin/help/automatic-tasks). | To run it now or change its time. |

## Tasks

### Send a request that gets solved quickly

Contact the platform team the way you usually do and write:

1. Your store's name.
2. What you want done, in one sentence.
3. The order number, product code, voucher or customer email it concerns.
4. When it happened, with date and time.
5. A screenshot, if you see an error message.

### Report an error message from the store

When something fails on the store, for example a review that is not saved or a payment that does not go through, the message says why. Most such messages also have a **Details** button (**Λεπτομέρειες** on the Greek store). Clicking it shows a **Code** and a **Request ID** in the message.

1. Click **Details**, or ask the customer who saw the message to do so.
2. Copy the **Code** and the **Request ID**, or take a screenshot that shows them.
3. Send them with your request, with the date and time.

The request ID lets the platform team find that exact attempt in the store's logs, so they do not have to reproduce it. A message without a **Details** button has nothing more to show.

## Things to know

- Never send passwords, yours or your customers'. The team does not need them.
- For anything about a customer's account, first make sure you are talking to that customer, for example with a call to the number they gave you.
- Screens such as the payment logs, ACS pickup lists or receipt counters are not in your menu. Only the platform team opens them.
