# Favourites

Your customers tap the heart on a product to keep it in their favourites. Here you see all those favourites: which customer, which product, when. It helps you see which products draw the most interest.

<span class="sidebar-path">Customers → Favourites</span>

## What you see

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **User** | The customer's email. |
| **Product** | The product's name. |
| **Created** | When they added it to their favourites. |

Search finds by customer email, product name or SKU (product code). In the filters you can pick a customer, a product, a product category or a date range.

Only customers with an account have favourites. A visitor who taps the heart is asked to sign in.

## Tasks

### See how many people want a product

1. Open <span class="sidebar-path">Customers → Favourites</span>.
2. Type the product's name or code in the search box.

The number of rows is the number of customers who have it in their favourites.

### See one customer's favourites

Type the customer's email in the search box.

### Remove a favourite on request

The customer removes it themselves, by tapping the heart again or from the **Favourites** page of their account. If they ask you to, select the row and delete it.

## Things to know

- **What the customer gets:** when the price of a favourite product drops, or when the product is back in stock after selling out, the customer sees a notification under **Notifications** in their account. They get no email for this. For emails there are [Product alerts](/admin/customers/product-alerts).
- **The price notification** starts when you change the product's own price or discount and its final price goes down.
- **To switch the feature off**, turn off the `FAVOURITES_ENABLED` setting under <span class="sidebar-path">Settings → Store Settings</span>. It is on from the start. While it is off, the hearts and the favourites page leave the store and no notifications are sent. Existing favourites are not deleted.
- **When a customer deletes their account**, their favourites are deleted too.
