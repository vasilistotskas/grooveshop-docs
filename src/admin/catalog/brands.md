# Brands

This is where you keep the names of the brands you sell. The brand shows above the product name and in the feeds (product files for Meta, TikTok and Google).

<span class="sidebar-path">Catalog → Brands</span>

## What you see

One row per brand with its **Name**, how many **Products** carry it and when it was created. Search looks in the name.

## Tasks

### Add a brand
1. Click **Add**.
2. Write the **Name** as customers should see it, e.g. "Philips".
3. Save.

### Give a product its brand
1. Open the product from [Products](/admin/catalog/products).
2. On the **Product Information** tab, type the first letters in **Brand** and pick it.
3. Save.

### Correct a brand's name
Open the brand, change the **Name** and save. The change shows on all its products.

## Things to know

- **The name is the same in every language.** Brands are not translated.
- **Each name exists once.** You cannot create a second brand with the same name.
- **In the store**: the brand shows on the product card and page, and the product list has a brand filter. The store's search does not look in the brand name; put it in the product name too if customers should find it that way.
- **In the feeds**: a product without a brand is sent to Meta, TikTok and Google with your store's name as the brand.
- **Deleting a brand does not delete products.** Its products are simply left without a brand.
