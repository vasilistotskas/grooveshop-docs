# Tags

Tags are short markers you put on products for your own use, e.g. "New season" or "To recheck". Customers do not see them. Blog posts have their own tags; see [Categories, authors & tags](/admin/content/blog/categories-authors-tags).

<span class="sidebar-path">Catalog → Tags</span>

## What you see

One row per tag, with its name, **Active** and **Usage** (on how many products). **Active** can be changed right in the list: tick it and click **Save**. Drag the rows to change the order.

The **Tag Status** filter finds, for example, the **Active Tags** or the ones used nowhere.

## Tasks

### Put a new tag on products
1. Click **Add**, write the **Label** in every language and save.
2. Open each product from [Products](/admin/catalog/products) and its **Tags** tab.
3. Add a row, pick the tag and save.

### Merge two tags
There is no merge button, and deleting cannot be undone. Work in this order:

1. Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type "Tagged Items" to open the list of every assignment.
2. Filter **Tag** to the one you will retire. You see every product that has it.
3. Open those products one by one and add the tag you keep on their **Tags** tab.
4. When you are done, delete the old tag. It leaves every product at once.

### Retire a tag
Untick **Active**. It stays on the products, so you can turn it back on later.

## Things to know

- **Customers never see these tags**, not on the product page, in lists or in filters. **Active** and the order only affect this screen.
- Product tags and blog tags are two separate lists and never mix.
- Deleting a tag removes it from every product.
