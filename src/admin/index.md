# Welcome

The admin is your store's back office. From here you manage products, orders, customers, shipping and settings. It opens at your store's address with `/admin/` at the end and only ever shows your own store's data.

If you are just starting, go to [First steps](/admin/getting-started/first-steps).

## Roles

Every member of your team has one of three roles.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | View, add and edit orders, products, customers, marketing, content, shipping, payment methods and VAT, and preview email templates. Cannot delete anything and does not see the store's settings. |
| **Admin** | Everything Staff can, plus deleting, **Store Profile**, **Team**, **Store Settings** and **Cache**. Cannot edit or remove an Owner. |
| **Owner** | Everything an Admin can, plus managing the other Owners. |

The platform team that hosts your store also sees a **System** section. Details in [Roles & team](/admin/getting-started/roles-and-team).

## What you see

### The sidebar

The sidebar shows only what concerns you and your store. The sections, in order:

- **Dashboard**: the period's figures and what is waiting. See [Dashboard](/admin/getting-started/dashboard).
- **Sales**: Orders, Receipts, Credit Documents, Carts. See [Orders](/admin/sales/orders).
- **Catalog**: Products, Categories, Brands, Attributes, Images, Tags, Reviews. See [Products](/admin/catalog/products).
- **Customers**: Customers, Addresses, Messages, Feedback, Favourites, Notifications, Wholesale, Loyalty, Newsletter. See [Users & addresses](/admin/customers/users-and-addresses).
- **Marketing**: Promotions, Gift Cards, Recommendations. See [Promotions](/admin/marketing/promotions).
- **Content**: Pages, Menus, Page Layouts, Blog. See [Pages](/admin/content/pages).
- **Shipping**: ACS Shipments, BoxNow Shipments, Pickup Points. See [The daily routine](/admin/shipping/daily-routine).
- **Settings**: Store Profile, Team, Store Settings, Payment Methods, Shipping Providers, VAT Rates, Email Templates, Cache.

An entry with a sub-menu opens when you click it. If only one page of the sub-menu concerns you, the entry opens that page directly.

### Why something is missing from the sidebar

- **Your role.** A screen your role cannot open is not shown at all.
- **Your plan.** Loyalty, Wholesale, Promotions, Gift Cards, Recommendations and Blog appear only when your plan includes them. If you downgraded and the feature still holds records (gift cards with a balance, for instance), those pages stay, read-only and marked **Off**, so you can still look things up.
- **Your settings.** A plan feature you have switched off stays in the sidebar marked **Off**, so you can set it up before customers see it, but only for Owners and Admins, who can switch it back on in Store Settings. Staff do not see an **Off** menu. Pages that only collect what customers send (Reviews, Feedback, Favourites, Newsletter, Blog Comments, Receipts, Credit Documents, Gift Cards, Points Transactions and the like) show while their feature is on, or while they hold something, and are marked **Off** when only the second is true.
- **A shop you have not opened.** With the catalogue or the cart switched off, Products and the other catalogue pages show an **Off** mark to Owners and Admins only, and Carts and Orders appear only if some already exist.
- **Your couriers.** The ACS or BoxNow pages appear only while that courier is active.

Every switch is on [Store settings](/admin/settings/store-settings).

### Numbers next to the sidebar

Six entries show a number. It counts work that is waiting and hides when it is zero.

| Entry | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | Orders in status **Pending**. |
| **Products** | Active products with 1 to 9 in stock. |
| **Reviews** | Reviews **Waiting for approval**. |
| **Messages** | Contact-form messages from the last 7 days. |
| **Wholesale** | Business-customer applications waiting for review. |
| **Blog** | Blog comments waiting for approval. |

Messages have no "read" mark. The number drops by itself as messages turn a week old.

### Top and bottom of the page

- The field at the top of the sidebar filters its entries. **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) also finds records. See [Search & command palette](/admin/getting-started/search-and-command-palette).
- Your name at the bottom of the sidebar opens your account: theme (**Light**, **Dark**, **System**), the admin language, **View site** (opens the store), **Change password** and **Log out**.
- The bottom of every page shows the platform **Version**. Quote it when you report a problem. The **Help & Guides** link to this guide is there too.

## Things to know

- Countries, regions, store addresses and the schedule of automatic tasks are managed by the platform team. See [When to contact support](/admin/help/contact-support).
- The admin language changes only your own view, not the store.
